## Writeup Contest Information
For UIUCTF 2025 we paid out $1,500 in writeup prizes. In total, we received 79 writeup submissions and awarded prizes to 26 writeups.

### Prize Information
- The **top 3** writeups were awarded **\$150**, **\$125**, and **\$100** respectively.
- The remaining prize pool was used to award quality writeups. This year, we decided to award winning writeups between **\$40-80**
- It is possible to win multiple writeup prizes; however, there is a maximum limit of **$200 per individual**.

### Eligible Writeups
Writeups were graded by **individual challenges** or **challenge suites**. Challenge suites are sets of related challenges that may require solving one before continuing to the next or use similar skills. Each suite is only eligible for one prize, regardless of the number of writeups created for challenges in a suite. The challenge suites for UIUCTF 2025 were:
- **osint / Mr. Blue Sky suite** (Mr. Blue Sky, Bad Blood, Age of Aquarius)
- **osint / Geoguesser Suite** (park, cherry_blossom)

### Scoring Procedure
Writeups were graded with three criteria in mind:
- Correctness and clarity
- Approachability and beginner-friendliness
- Presentation

Writeups were scored between 1 to 10, with 10 being the highest quality and 1 being the least. Our event staff scored writeups and the final scores were the average of these ratings.
